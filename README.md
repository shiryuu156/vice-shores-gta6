**Vice Shores** is an open-world crime game built in three.js. You are Rook, a getaway driver working the sun-bleached streets of Vice Shores, a Leonida-inspired coastal city. Take jobs from your fixer, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, and keep moving as day turns to night across the city.

## How to play

- **W A S D** — move on foot, drive in a car
- **Mouse** — look around
- **Shift** — sprint
- **Space** — jump / handbrake
- **F** — steal a car, exit a car, punch
- **Click** — shoot · **R** — reload
- **E** — take a job at a marker
- **Esc** — pause · **M** — mute

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser. No build step and no dependencies — the game runs from its own files.
